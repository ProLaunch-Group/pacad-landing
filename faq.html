01 — PROLAUNCH ACADEMY

General Academy & Courses

ProLaunch Academy (a subsidiary of ProLaunch Group) is a digital skills and technology training institution offering practical, industry-aligned bootcamps, short courses, and certification programmes in both tech and non-tech disciplines. Our goal is to help ambitious individuals build job-ready skills, earn credentials, and launch their careers.

We currently offer 9 practical training programmes across two durations:

  • 12-Week Intensive Bootcamps: Software Development, Cloud Engineering, and Data Analytics.
  • 6-Week Career Courses: SEO Marketing, Social Media Marketing, Email Marketing, Graphics Design, WordPress Development, and Technical Support.

Regular tuition fees depend on the programme duration:

  • 6-Week Courses: ₦30,000
  • 12-Week Courses: ₦60,000

For eligible applicants under our 500 Fully Funded Scholarship Initiative, training fees for the four scholarship tracks are 100% covered.

No prior experience is required! Our programmes are structured to take beginners from foundational concepts to practical, real-world projects step by step. All you need is commitment, curiosity, and a willingness to learn.

Students earn verified, sharable digital milestone badges as they hit key learning milestones (great for LinkedIn profiles) and receive a Certificate of Completion upon finishing their programme. Additionally, Cloud Engineering students receive a massive voucher discount for the official AWS Cloud Practitioner exam plus Double Certification upon bootcamp completion.
